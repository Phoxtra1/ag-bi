# AG-BI — Decision-useful information discovery and research standard

## Purpose

AG-BI is not simply a search interface or a directory of links. Its five pillars describe an end-to-end information service:

1. **Discover** relevant information, records and leads.
2. **Analyze and assess** meaning, quality, context, patterns, relationships and competing explanations.
3. **Verify** material claims against evidence actually accessed and examined.
4. **Organize and connect** information into coherent findings, timelines and relationships.
5. **Present the outcome** in a clear, traceable and useful form.

The commercial value is the work performed across these pillars. Customers are not paying Amineva Global for ownership of public information or for links that they could open themselves. They are paying for the research process and the resulting structured work product.

## Minimum standard for a paid report

Scale the depth to the question and the work completed. Where relevant, a report should contain:

- A direct executive answer.
- Key findings, prioritised by importance.
- Research scope and methodology: what was searched, what was actually examined, and what was not available.
- Claim-by-claim evidence assessment for material claims.
- Source provenance: author or issuing institution, title, date, source type, original URL, retrieval/review status and the limits of what that source establishes.
- Analysis of context, patterns, relationships, contradictions and plausible alternative explanations.
- Evidence gaps, uncertainty and what further evidence would strengthen or change the conclusion.
- Practical implications or decision considerations relevant to the customer's question.
- A balanced conclusion and useful next steps.

Do not force every component into a simple request. Do not generate generic filler to make a report appear more substantial.

## Evidence and traceability rules

- A search result or catalogue entry is a lead, not proof.
- A source may be listed as supporting a claim only if the relevant content was actually accessed and examined and it genuinely supports that claim.
- Catalogue metadata can establish details about the catalogue entry, but not the truth of claims in an underlying work.
- Distinguish source authenticity, institutional authority, factual accuracy and independent corroboration.
- Distinguish facts, official statements, allegations, reported claims, analysis, inference and unknowns.
- Use explicit assessment labels where appropriate: Verified, Corroborated, Partially supported, Disputed, Unverified, Contradicted, Inconclusive and Requires further confirmation.
- Never invent sources, quotations, URLs, dates, record identifiers or claim-to-source relationships.
- If evidence is insufficient, state that plainly. An honest evidence gap is preferable to an unsupported conclusion.

## What customers are buying

The value proposition is the combination of:
- discovery across relevant information and sources;
- assessment of relevance, reliability and limitations;
- verification work where source material is accessible;
- organisation and connection of fragmented information;
- transparent references and evidence trails;
- analysis, synthesis and decision-useful conclusions.

Public source links should remain visible and traceable. AG-BI's value is not hiding those links; it is doing the careful work of finding, examining, comparing, contextualising and presenting information responsibly.

## Pricing tier discipline

- **Quick Discovery:** focused answer, concise context and limited source discovery.
- **Insight Report:** structured multi-part analysis, meaningful assessment and clearly explained findings.
- **Deep Research:** broad and complex work involving substantial multi-source examination, cross-checking, timelines, relationships or competing accounts.
- **Sensitive Info:** separate sensitive public-interest mode, subject to its stricter privacy, evidence and human-review requirements.

Assign the tier according to the work actually completed, not the customer's wording or a desire to charge more. Never imply a level of verification or source review that did not occur.

## Current Phase 1 limitation

The active Library of Congress connector currently retrieves catalogue metadata and record links only. It does not automatically download or examine the underlying documents. Therefore, these results must be labelled as discovery leads. They must not be cited as proof of substantive claims until the relevant underlying content has actually been reviewed. Other registered repositories remain credentials-required or manual-review only until their access and reuse conditions are satisfied.

**Operating principle:** AG-BI earns trust and payment by making information more useful, understandable and assessable — while showing exactly what the evidence does and does not establish.
